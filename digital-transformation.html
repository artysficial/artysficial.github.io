Approach

Rolling out a new tool with minimal disruption.

How I approach introducing technology into a working operation.

  1. 1 · Learn the current process

    Map how work is done today in BPMN, with the people who do it, before proposing anything.

  2. 2 · Integrate, don't replace

    Connect the new tool to existing systems through APIs or data exports, so nothing that works today breaks.

  3. 3 · Pilot side by side

    Run the new way next to the old one on a small scope, compare results and fix gaps early.

  4. 4 · Train in plain language

    Short guides and hands-on help for colleagues less familiar with digital tools, with a go-to person on each team.

  5. 5 · Measure and improve

    Track time saved, errors avoided and usage on a dashboard, and share it so the benefit is visible.
