About me

I am a quality engineering professional with nearly four years of experience assuring mission-critical banking and payment security technology for central banks and government clients across four continents. Publications

Journal article

Published 22 May 2024

Implementation of the Graphic Rating Scale in Determining Motorcycle Indent Priorities at Motorcycle Dealers

Implementasi Graphic Rating Scale Dalam Menentukan Prioritas Indent Motor Pada Dealer Sepeda Motor

Denny Riandhita Arief Permana, Muhamad Fahrulrozi, Ahlan Ismono, Rahmadani Tyas Ningrum

Technomedia Journal (TMJ), Vol. 9, No. 1, June 2024

PT Cheger Motor, an authorised Honda motorcycle dealer in South Tangerang, handled indent (pre-order) sales through manual ledgers and WhatsApp, leading to booking delays, lost records and redundant data. This research develops an integrated information system that stores and manages indent orders and calculates a priority value for each transaction using the Graphic Rating Scale method, so the dealer can rank the queue against defined criteria.
